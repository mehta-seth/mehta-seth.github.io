I recently got interested in art history after seeing Monet’s and Renoir’s paintings of La Grenouillère side by side, and decided to teach myself using works that are publicly available. It’s by no means the complete works of any of the artists in it, just me familiarising myself with them.

The works are grouped by movement, and each has a short note on why it matters and what to look for.
