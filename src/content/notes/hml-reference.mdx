_This is the help-me-learn reference note: the layout and every component in one place, so a new note can be built by copying from here. The words and equations are placeholders and mean nothing yet._

Every note here sets out to explain one difficult idea in the most intuitive way it can: a plain-language account, a real analogy where one helps, a figure where a picture says more than a paragraph, and never at the cost of getting the mathematics right. The aim is simple. Someone meeting a topic for the first time should find the explanation they wish they had been given.

The paragraphs below are placeholder text, here only so the typography can be seen at the width these notes use. None of it means anything yet.
